# Global Chocolate Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes global chocolate sales performance across multiple countries, brands, and product categories using Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques.

The analysis focuses on:
- revenue performance
- profitability drivers
- product demand
- inventory optimization
- operational risk
- seasonal trends
- country-level growth analysis

The objective was to identify high-performing markets, profitable products, operational inefficiencies, and strategic business opportunities through data-driven insights.

# Business Objectives

The analysis aimed to answer key business questions such as:

- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- Which markets are underperforming despite having many stores?
- Which regions show the strongest sales growth?
- Which products should receive higher inventory allocation?
- Which products demonstrate seasonal vs stable demand?
- Which factors most strongly influence profitability?
- Which markets require targeted marketing campaigns?
- Which products perform differently across countries?
- What are the biggest operational risks?

# Key Insights

- Canada generated the highest total revenue (~$5.09M)
- France showed the strongest sales growth (+0.9%)
- Germany recorded the weakest growth and lowest revenue performance
- Revenue (97%) and quantity sold (71%) were the strongest drivers of profitability
- Discounts negatively impacted profit (-12%)
- Dark Chocolate 50% and Truffle Chocolate 80% demonstrated stable year-round demand
- Premium chocolate products showed the strongest inventory allocation potential
- Canada and UK had higher operational complexity despite strong revenue performance

# Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory allocation for high-demand premium products
- Reduce dependence on aggressive discounting
- Expand in high-growth markets such as France and Australia
- Improve operational efficiency in Canada and UK
- Increase targeted marketing in underperforming regions
