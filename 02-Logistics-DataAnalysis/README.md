# **Supply Chain & Logistics Delay Analysis Dashboard**

## **Project Overview**
This project analyzes global supply chain shipment performance using **Power BI** to identify operational bottlenecks, delay patterns, transportation inefficiencies, and cost optimization opportunities.

The dashboard provides interactive insights into shipment delays, port congestion, customs issues, carrier performance, and regional logistics trends to support data-driven operational decisions.

## **Business Problem**
Supply chain delays increase operational costs, impact customer satisfaction, and reduce delivery performance.

### **Project Objectives**
- Identify major causes of shipment delays  
- Analyze delay trends across regions and transportation modes  
- Evaluate carrier and port performance  
- Measure operational impact on cost and delivery efficiency  
- Recommend optimization opportunities to improve on-time delivery

## **Key Insights**
- Approximately **66% of shipments experienced delays**, with an average delay duration of **3.75 days**
- **Trans-Pacific** and **Asia-Europe** routes contributed the highest share of delayed shipments
- **Port congestion** and **customs documentation issues** emerged as the leading operational bottlenecks
- Reactive strategies such as rerouting showed significantly higher delay severity compared to proactive planning measures
- Analysis identified opportunities to:
  - Reduce delays by **15–20%**
  - Improve on-time delivery performance
  - Lower operational costs through process optimization
